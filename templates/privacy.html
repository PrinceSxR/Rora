Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as session cookies needed to keep you signed in.

We do not connect to your bank accounts or collect card details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store, display, and summarise your expenses.
  • Keep the service secure and working properly.
  • Contact you about important changes to your account or these policies.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your information is stored in a secured database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account at any time, after which your personal and expense data will be removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties, except where required by law. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when serving them.

5. Your Rights

You can view, edit, or delete your expense data at any time from your account. You may also ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team.
